VERIFICATION, and this is where round 7 was sent back:
Round 7 was delivered as complete and two of its four jobs were not. Both failures were one step
sideways from the fixture the patch was written for — a sequence diagram with no `participant` lines
(which is how most people write one) reported "0 participants" beside a picture of three people, and
a chip whose sentence was composed once still promised branch colours over a pie chart. The numbers
on the chosen fixtures were all green.

So: test one step sideways from your own fixture, every time. Ordinary input, not hostile input — a
comment line, a missing declaration, a second diagram opened within nine seconds. And a check that
finds nothing on both the patched build and the base has established nothing; say so out loud rather
than counting it green.

Where a fix is visual, render it and look at the picture before reporting. Two defects in the last
release were found only that way and neither was visible in any failing number.

THE STANDING RULE, which two of round 7's jobs existed to serve:
Nothing in this app may promise what it cannot deliver. A tooltip, a chip, a count, a menu label or a
disabled state that overstates is a defect of the same kind as a crash. A confidently wrong number is
worse than an obviously wrong one.

If a job cannot be done honestly as briefed, say so and deliver the rest.
