THE ONE THING THAT WILL WASTE YOUR ROUND IF YOU MISS IT:
A <meta> CSP cannot be relaxed at runtime and cannot usefully be rewritten after parse. It CAN be
intersected - a second policy added before anything loads applies alongside the first, strictest
wins. A probe that checks the meta's content attribute will report a rewrite as working when the
browser is still enforcing the original. Read the violation, not the string.
