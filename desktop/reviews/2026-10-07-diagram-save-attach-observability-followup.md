## More precise source-supported candidate, still unproven

**A temporarily hidden transfer control being restored by asynchronous refresh can shift Save horizontally in the shared flex header.** This is a testable mechanism, not an assertion that it happened in the original run.

`src/ui/windows/diagram-transfer.js:5–6` inserts `transferView` before Close, initially hidden. Refresh at16/20/27 hides it after a refused/missing own-view or shelf result or exception; successful refresh at22 makes it visible and sets “Attach to workspace” or “Detach window”. At39 refresh runs on ready and every1,200ms. `src/ui/diagram/window.html:2` defines the header as wrapping flex with an expanding h1 and8px gap; hidden elements have `display:none!important`. Reintroducing a trailing control can reduce available h1 space and move Save left without changing Save's size or y. This direction matches the original observation. **The original trace does not retain transfer width, so139.90625px is not proven equal to transfer width plus gap.**

This candidate is more specific than “some layout changed”: it identifies an actual hide/show branch, asynchronous refresh and a layout dependency. However, no original getView/getShelf refusal, visibility mutation or refresh timestamp was captured. `registry.caller`/`#live` and dock reads do not simply revoke a valid caller because the roster is frozen (`src/windows/registry.mjs:238–254,607–624`; `src/windows/dock-ipc.mjs:10–23`). Therefore the preceding invalid-field Lock rollback alone is **not** proof that this hide branch executed. A live result, identity or shelf transition still has to be observed.

Additional unproven alternatives remain a native viewport/bounds transition, another header visibility/text change, or delayed shared-shell/font/layout initialization. Shared shell (`src/ui/shared/shell.js:42–63`) hides the local theme selector once at build, applies theme metrics and polls/resumes appearance. Chrome changes button metrics and small-window wrapping. Those paths are relevant observation sites, but no original font/theme/viewport transition was recorded; initial startup succeeded, and the once-only theme-selector hiding must not be blamed as a post-Lock event without its timestamp. Colour or radius changes alone do not establish a140px horizontal displacement.

The point-to-dispatch observation gap is concrete: exact-original `tests/native/attach-page.mjs:17` waits for rectangle stability, separately evaluates a clipped/hit-tested point, then awaits two separate CDP mouse calls. Current `tests/native/pointer.mjs:2–21` observes two rAFs and finite animations; it cannot guarantee geometry remains unchanged after its returned point. That helper's hash was not recorded in the original receipt. This gap permits the competing hypotheses; it does not prove CDP coordinate corruption or justify retries.
