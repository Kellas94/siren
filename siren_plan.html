T-Industries SIREN · consolidated plan

Everything worth building, from four investigations

Two verification passes over held rounds, one interface investigation, and one outside competitive analysis. Deduplicated, ranked, and cut down to what changes something. The ordering rule is the standing one — anything that loses a person's work or says something the app cannot deliver comes before anything that is merely better.

25 August 2026 live is v1.70.0 rounds 11 and 12 held 27 items, five sections

The decision taken today

Two builds. The offline one does not contain the external host at all.

Not opt-in, not default-on — absent from the code and from the policy, so a firm's IT department can verify it in thirty seconds by reading one line. ELK leaves that build with it. A second build, labelled Connected, keeps it.

Two independent routes arrived here at once. The outside analysis said an opt-in switch is not procurement-true — “no requests on the normal path” and “cannot phone home” are different claims. The verification then found that the opt-in switch does not even work: with any fragment in the URL it silently does nothing while writing the opposite preference, so somebody leaving strict mode is told on screen that they are still protected and the protection drops by itself at the next reload. Deleting the host deletes three of the five blockers instead of repairing them.

Loses work, or says what it cannot do first these come before everything, including the interface

Every one of these is measured on a real build, with a real gesture. Six of the nine are held rounds; three are older than either.

  1. Seven destructive actions were repainted as safe

    Delete class and six siblings went from red to the primary blue while the action permanently destroys the class name you typed and every block assignment to it. Undo is disabled after a reload and no restore point holds it. Only one of the seven was ever driven; two of the untested ones can hold substantial typed content.

    round 12
  2. A document marker that stops being the block

    With connect mode armed, clicking the target block's top-right corner destroys the connection you were drawing, opens Docs, and leaves the mode armed — no toast, no error. Double-click rename, Ctrl+click and drag die at the same pixel. On a state-diagram node that corner is a twelfth of the whole node.

    round 11
  3. A fill colour discarded without a word

    Set a colour in the Style panel, click the block to look at it, and the colour is gone. Two editors, two commit models — the inspector commits on change, the panel wants “Apply”. The app says two things at that moment; neither mentions what it just threw away.

    interface
  4. “Merging saves the target first.” It does not.

    The snapshot written is of the diagram on screen, not of the one about to be overwritten. The one path that genuinely loses writing is also the one promising protection it does not provide. Cheapest honest fix is one line of text; the proper fix is to snapshot the target.

    round 12
  5. A navigation key edits the document

    Bare Tab inside the preview appends a block to your Mermaid source. It is on this project's own acceptance list — no standard navigation key modifies data — and it is how the keyboard route to the tour damages a diagram on the way there.

    pre-existing
  6. “Go to this block”, where no block exists

    Offered and enabled on four of seven diagram types, reached through the app's own picker. The click closes Docs, opens the diagram and stops. The old build said “Open this diagram” and did exactly that, so the false promise is new.

    round 11
  7. The first-run tour cannot be escaped or announced

    It no longer eats what you type — that fix is excellent and stays. But Escape does nothing, it costs 77 Tab presses, a screen reader is never told it exists, and because typing no longer walks it to the end it returns at every launch.

    round 11
  8. PowerPoint labels run outside their own shapes

    Every diamond, cylinder, pill and edge label lost line wrapping — 37pt out of each edge on an ordinary two-node diagram the old build wrapped correctly. A working case broken while fixing a broken one, and the brief that caused it was mine.

    round 11
  9. Following a reference does not bring the block on screen

    The inspector opens on a block the canvas never scrolled to, so the panel names something invisible and floats over the Build panel. Fails on a five-block flowchart in a 1024-wide window, not just on large ones.

    round 11

The interface, structurally then clutter was fought by depth; fight it by context instead

Depth-hiding never asks whether a control applies, only whether it fits. So it took away the things used every session and kept every control that does nothing.

  1. Make the Style panel type-true — hide, do not disable

    112 of 260 type-and-control pairs change nothing, and 79 of those give no sign at all. Render only what bites: 13 controls on a flowchart, 5 on a sequence, 3 on C4. The gate is per control, not per fold, or the lie is rebuilt one level down. The lookup table is the matrix already measured.

    interface
  2. A flat Look bar — three controls, nothing to open

    Answered on 26 August: three, personally. That fires the condition the investigation set, and settles it — a flat row wins outright at three, so the expandable chip bar is out. About 40px, zero clicks, no tray, nothing to learn. The prototype at prototypes/look_bar.html stays as evidence of what was compared, not as the thing to build.

    Still removes what it was going to remove: a nested fold level, the hidden ⚙ Style button and the rule that hides it, the Inspect menu row, and the duplicate flow-direction control. One thing still open: WHICH three. That is now the only question between here and building it.

    interface
  3. One control per verb

    Two block editors with two commit models. Two controls named Guided. A command palette harvesting 229 buttons of which 181 are not on screen. Each hidden control needed a second route to justify hiding it, and each second route became a duplicate that can disagree.

    interface
  4. Ambient motion needs a stop

    Not taste — an obligation. Motion that runs more than five seconds beside other content must have a pause, stop or hide control. Several themes animate continuously behind the diagram.

    outside

What makes the offline claim provable then the moat is only a moat if a stranger can verify it

“One file, offline, no account” is the strongest thing this product has. It is currently a sentence rather than evidence.

  1. Split the builds

    Offline: no external URL in the code or the policy, ELK removed. Connected: labelled, distributed separately, keeps it. This deletes the toggle rather than fixing it.

    decided
  2. Every release gets an identity

    Version, published hash, dependency and licence manifest, release notes — and the version stamped inside every project file and every export, so an engagement file records exactly which build produced it. A mutable HTML file is convenient and weak evidence: anyone can change one line and still call it 1.70.0.

    outside
  3. Export conformance becomes a release gate

    Per diagram type and format, published as native / mixed / image: shape count, picture count, diacritics, connectors, fonts, re-open. Most of the instrument exists — it runs 19 of 25 today. What is missing is that it gates the release and that its verdict is printed where a buyer can read it.

    outside
  4. Say what is stored on the machine, and offer to purge it

    “No server” is not “no persistence”. Client material in browser storage, snapshots and temporary exports is a retention and device-loss question. It needs a visible explanation, a true session mode and a reliable purge.

    outside

Honesty in the names cheap, and overdue

  1. “Workpapers” overstates what Docs is

    Typed prepared by and reviewed by fields are not authenticated sign-off. Without identity, immutable review history, locking and retention this is a documentation and workpaper-drafting surface. Our own rule, applied to a product name rather than a button.

    outside
  2. The Guide contradicts the product

    It tells the reader that other diagram types need a renderer “which loads when internet access is available”, and to save mermaid.min.js beside the file. Neither is true — the renderer travels inside it. Two differently-named offline flags are on screen at once.

    round 12
  3. The under-9pt export warning never renders where it matters

    The status bar ellipsis falls before the number, so the one actionable fact is cut. The figure itself is a clamp presented as a measurement — 44 blocks and 120 blocks both report “1.0pt”. And it arrives in a success-styled toast.

    round 11
  4. Two key presses delete a reference with no confirmation

    The menu focuses the first enabled row, and on a dangling chip that is Remove.

    round 11

Deferred, each with a reason not now

These are recommendations, not defects. They are the owner's calls and none of them blocks anything above.

  1. Tier the twenty diagram types

    Five or six deeply supported, the rest structured-export or raw compatibility. Keeps the breadth without claiming all twenty are equally products. Deferred because it is a positioning decision, and the naming work already done removed the worst of the overclaim.

    outside
  2. Cut the theme count

    The recommendation is 39 down to about six. Deferred, and worth arguing about. The analysis is right that themes are not why anyone buys this. It is measuring the wrong thing if the question is whether the tool is a pleasure to open — and that is a real question, not decoration. Keep them; stop adding.

    outside
  3. Shrink presentation mode

    Your strongest export already opens in PowerPoint as editable shapes. Let PowerPoint do presentation authoring; keep sequencing, full-screen and notes.

    outside
  4. Freeze the broad Guided editor

    Do not spend another month making every Mermaid grammar clickable across sixteen types. An excellent process-map editor beats a mediocre generic one.

    outside
  5. Pause the hosted general-audience version

    Remove “without sending the material to a server” and the remaining proposition competes with Miro and Eraser without collaboration, integrations or an ecosystem. The two editions do not currently share a moat.

    outside
  6. The pre-existing reference-picker problems

    It offers TD, the word mindmap, a journey title and ER relationship labels as though they were blocks — and never offers ER entities, which do render. Behind the honesty fix above, not in front of it.

    round 11
